# Bounded hazard cue scale

JUMP / DODGE / SLIDE were perspective-scaled to roughly5.7 nominal font-box pixels at12m on844x390, despite about0.91 seconds to the near contact band at Story speed12. This is projected font-box geometry, not glyph body size, a human reaction test or an accessibility certification.

Keep the existing cue words, local positions, upright rotation, font and depth occlusion. Scale each cue to at least16 nominal projected font-box pixels, capped at4 times its original scale. Never shrink a naturally larger near cue. Refresh after entity motion and viewport changes, including paused resize. Do not change spawn cadence, field of view, collisions, action windows, rewards, saves or controls.

Controlled actual native Full-quality stills of low/Meadow, tall/Dunes, beam/Grove at24/12/6/3m in portrait and compact landscape showed the improvement.
