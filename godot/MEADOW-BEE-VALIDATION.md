# Meadow bee dodge requirement, November 10, 2026

Story Meadow's opening instructions and STORYLINE.md call for dodging bees, but its old spawn branch produced only bales. After the first four seconds, each Meadow wave now has a 25% bee-swarm chance; otherwise it stays a bale. This is a conservative initial mix, not accepted human difficulty or fun tuning. Other modes/worlds, collision rules, speed, hearts, save formats and checkpoint progression are unchanged. The existing tall/sway/DODGE path handles the swarm.

Three uniformly scaled Quaternius Bee models make a narrow vertical swarm spanning the existing tall dodge envelope. The static, friendly bee was chosen over the heavier animated Bee Enemy monster. Publisher: https://poly.pizza/m/HvfIku26CK . CC0 provenance, exact hash and adaptations are in assets/models/meadow_bee/LICENSE.txt. The unmodified GLB is 57,672 bytes, 914 triangles and two surfaces with an embedded atlas. The swarm is 2,742 triangles/six surfaces with shared imported resources. No new animations or paid assets. One-command recovery now restores this model from hash-verified repository-contained base64. Credits name and link it.
