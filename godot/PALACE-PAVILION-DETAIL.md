# Palace pavilion detail study

Original sparse stepped bracket arms and teal/gold eave bands now support the three side pavilions. Static pavilion geometry is grouped by material to avoid adding tiny draw calls. On wide landscape screens the pavilions move outward to separate the roof and moon silhouettes; portrait and short-landscape positions retain their existing layout.

Reference only, no photo pixels or other game media imported:
- Architectura Sinica's bracket terminology and column/eave relationship: https://architecturasinica.org/keyword/k000019
- Actual Beihai Five Dragons Pavilion photo by Reinhold Möller, viewed for layered eaves, beam bands and restrained supporting detail: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Peking_Beihai_Park_Five_dragon_Pavilion-20110104-RM-110807.jpg . The photograph is CC BY-SA4.0 and is not included in the game.
- Earlier official Sky/Journey studies remain composition references, documented in the research ledger.

This is a simplified original fantasy setting, not a reconstruction or a historically accurate bracket system. Outer-sky moon and center-shifted portrait moon trials were rejected; moving wide pavilions retained the existing moon group and clearer destination framing.
