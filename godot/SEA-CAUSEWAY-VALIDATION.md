# Storm Sea stone causeway

Source preparation, November 6, 2026. Not a hosted deployment or actual-device performance approval.

## Change
An original low stone causeway makes the route read as a built crossing rather than a dark strip painted over water. Sea-only cool grey paving replaces the water-like lane surface. Two low coping strips and sixty short posts are one mesh surface, using the existing CC0 nature rock texture. No new asset or external dependency. Existing sea rocks, storm water, sky, small moon, turtle, wave and lightning hazards, crossing rails, lane positions, timing, collision, story and saves are unchanged. Light retains the previous fallback.

This is modest environmental grounding, not a complete Chinese Sea redesign. Low coping and sparse posts evoke a stone crossing but do not establish a specific historical structure. The route remains straight and surrounding ocean sparse.
